HydroTwin VR · US river gauges
Real USGS daily flow and airport weather, Oct 2016–Sep 2026 (10 years).
Each station scene shows 12 forecast models (Gaussian processes with classical and quantum kernels, quantum and classical LSTMs and Transformers, persistence) on days they were not trained on (Oct 2023–Sep 2026).
Desktop: drag to look around · hover a pin for details · click a pin to open that station in 3D (real terrain, rivers, watershed, geology).
Headset: press Enter VR · point at a pin · trigger to open it · left stick move · right stick turn.
Pin height = latest flow (log scale); colour = latest flow vs that gauge's own median for the period.
